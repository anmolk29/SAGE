## Safe wording for the project

The October 2026 source list shows that the listed journal/conference sources cover the publication years for all 20 references. One source, *IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking*, is now inactive, but its coverage through 2024 includes the 2022 item. This evidence supports saying **“the venue/source was covered by Scopus for the cited publication year as of the October 2026 list.”** It does not support marking an individual article “Scopus-indexed” until its exact title or DOI is found in Scopus. Scopus documentation distinguishes source coverage years from document-level search results.
