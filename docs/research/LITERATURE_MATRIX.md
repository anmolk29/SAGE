## Synthesis and project position

The literature already covers: (a) edge-video architectures and resource trade-offs [1–6,19,20]; (b) sensor-aware collection/scheduling [7,15]; (c) video anomaly datasets/evaluation [9–12,17]; and (d) industrial image anomaly benchmarks [13,14,16]. Therefore, SAGE must not claim the first adaptive frame sampler, the first sensor-guided video monitor, or a new general industrial anomaly detector.

The defensible project contribution is a transparent, quota-matched comparison of four policies plus controlled miss/false-trigger/delay sensitivity, with per-clip records and explicit measurement boundaries. That contribution becomes empirical only after a dataset with suitable rights, temporal continuity, and valid event annotations is inspected and run. Current HOG person detection is semantically incompatible with general machine anomaly labels, and current cue simulation derives from event starts; both constraints belong in the presentation and manuscript.

## Scopus statement

The official October 2026 Scopus source list includes the cited journal/conference venues for each item's publication year. For journals, source IDs, status, and coverage years are recorded in [`SCOPUS_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`](SCOPUS_SOURCE_AUDIT.md); proceedings entries are listed by year/EID. One journal source is now inactive but was covered during the publication year of the cited paper. Elsevier explains that a source's coverage years show which years are available, while exact documents must be checked in Scopus search results. Until an exact DOI/title lookup is completed, use the wording “venue covered by Scopus for the publication year” and do not label an individual paper “Scopus indexed.”
